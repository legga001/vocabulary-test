A1 grammar

This, that, these and those

Use this and these for things near you. Use that and those for things farther away. These and those go with plurals.

The pattern

this/that + one thing; these/those + more than one

Work through an example

  1. Imagine holding two apples.
  2. They’re close and there are two, so choose these.
  3. Say “These apples are for lunch.”

More examples

This hat is mine. Those boots over there are yours.

  • This seat is free.
  • That cloud looks like a dog.
  • These keys are mine.
  • Those houses look old.

What to watch for

  • Use this and that with an amount, too: “This water is cold.”
  • On the phone, “This is Imani” introduces you.

Your turn

Point to two faraway bikes: “___ bikes are red.”

Reveal the answer

Those bikes are red. Use those for several things farther away.
