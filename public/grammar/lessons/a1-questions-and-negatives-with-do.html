A1 grammar

Questions and negatives with do

Use do or does before the subject in present-simple questions with most verbs. Use don’t or doesn’t for negatives. After does or doesn’t, use the base verb.

Let’s talk it through

Mr Fox says Think of do and does as helpers. Once you choose does, leave the action word in its plain form. The helper already tells us who you are talking about.

The pattern

do/does + subject + base verb?; subject + don’t/doesn’t + base verb

How does it work?

  1. Start with “Rosa likes pizza.”
  2. Choose does because Rosa is she.
  3. Remove s from likes and ask “Does Rosa like pizza?”

More examples

Does she play football? She doesn’t play on Tuesdays.

  • Do the buses stop here?
  • They don’t stop here on Sundays.
  • Where does Max work?
  • Max doesn’t work here now.

What to watch for

  • With be, ask “Is Max here?” without do.
  • If who is the person doing the action, ask “Who lives here?” with no do or does.
