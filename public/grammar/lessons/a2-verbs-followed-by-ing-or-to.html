A2 grammar

Verbs followed by -ing or to

Some verbs take an -ing form after them, such as enjoy. Others, such as want, take to plus the base verb. Learn the second form with the first verb.

The pattern

enjoy/finish/avoid + -ing; want/hope/decide + to + base verb

Work through an example

  1. You like the activity of swimming: choose “I enjoy swimming.”
  2. You have a goal for tomorrow: choose “I want to swim tomorrow.”
  3. The first verb tells you which form to use.

More examples

I enjoy cooking. I want to make pizza.

  • She finished packing.
  • We decided to walk.
  • I hope to see you soon.
  • Avoid touching the hot pan.

What to watch for

  • Like and love can often take either form: “I love cooking” and “I love to cook.”
  • After a preposition, an action verb usually ends in -ing: “I’m good at drawing.”

Your turn

Complete “Milo decided ___ (stay) at home.”

Reveal the answer

Milo decided to stay at home. Decide takes to plus the verb.
