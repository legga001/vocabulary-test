A1 grammar

Can and can’t

Use can plus the base verb for ability or permission. For a question, move can before the subject.

The pattern

can/can’t + base verb; can + subject + base verb?

Work through an example

  1. Start with “I open the window.”
  2. To ask permission, put can before I.
  3. Ask “Can I open the window?”

More examples

I can swim. I can’t dive. Can I sit here?

  • We can hear the music.
  • He can’t cook rice yet.
  • Can Luca help us? Yes, he can.
  • You can sit by the window.

What to watch for

  • Can stays the same with he, she and it.
  • The next verb has no to: “She can dance.”

Your turn

Fix “He can to skate.”

Reveal the answer

He can skate. Put the base verb straight after can.
