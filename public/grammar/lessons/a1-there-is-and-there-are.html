A1 grammar

There is and there are

Use there is to say one thing exists and there are for several things. For a question, start with is or are.

Let’s talk it through

Mr Fox says Use there is or there are when you want someone to know what they can find in a place. Look at the thing after the verb to see if it is singular or plural.

The pattern

there is + singular noun or amount; there are + plural noun

How does it work?

1. Use there are for several things

  1. You see two cups on a shelf.
  2. Choose are because cups is plural.
  3. Say “There are two cups on the shelf.”

2. Use there is for one thing or an amount

Choose there is with one chair → “There is a chair by the window.” Use the same pattern with milk → “There is milk in the fridge.”

3. Ask what is there

Start with “There are two shops.” Move are before there → “Are there two shops?”

More examples

There’s a ball in my bag. Are there any boots in yours?

  • There’s milk in the fridge.
  • There isn’t a lift.
  • Are there any clean towels?
  • There are three seats left.

What to watch for

  • In careful writing, use “there are two”, even if you hear “there’s two” in conversation.
  • “It is cold” describes the weather. “There is ice” says ice exists.
