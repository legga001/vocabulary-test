B1 grammar

Reported statements

Use say or tell to pass on a message. Tell usually takes a person as its object. After a past reporting verb, present forms often shift into the past, though an idea that is still true can stay in the present.

The pattern

say + (that) clause; tell + person + (that) clause

Work through an example

  1. Jo says “I can’t come today.”
  2. Report it later with “Jo told me”. Change I to she.
  3. Choose “Jo told me she couldn’t come that day” if you’re speaking on a different day.

More examples

“I’m tired,” said Jo. Jo told me she was tired.

  • Ana said she was late.
  • Ana told me she was late.
  • The coach says practice starts at six.
  • He said he had lost his wallet.

What to watch for

  • You can often leave out that.
  • If the message is still true or still applies, a present form can work: “She said the lift is broken.”
  • Use “said to me” or “told me”.

Your turn

Fix “Nina told she liked the film.”

Reveal the answer

Nina said she liked the film. With told, add a person: “Nina told us she liked the film.”
