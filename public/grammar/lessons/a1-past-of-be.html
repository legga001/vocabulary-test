A1 grammar

Past of be

Use was with I, he, she and it. Use were with you, we and they. Put the verb first to ask a question.

Let’s talk it through

Mr Fox says Was and were let you talk about where you were or how you felt before now. For a question, move the past form of be to the front.

The pattern

I/he/she/it + was; you/we/they + were

How does it work?

  1. Start with “The shops were open.”
  2. Add not after were for a negative: “The shops weren’t open.”
  3. Move were to the front for a question: “Were the shops open?”

More examples

I was at the park. Were you there too?

  • I was tired after lunch.
  • The film wasn’t scary.
  • We were in France last year.
  • Where were you at seven?

What to watch for

  • You takes were for one person or several people.
  • Use was/were without did in these questions.
