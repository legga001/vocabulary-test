B2 grammar

Articles and generalisations

Use plural or uncountable nouns without the when talking generally. Use the for a specific group or amount the reader can identify.

The pattern

general plural/amount: no article; specific noun: the; one example: a/an

Work through an example

  1. Start with the general idea “Books can be expensive.”
  2. Point to a specific group: “The books on that shelf are expensive.”
  3. Pick one example: “A book can make a good gift.”

More examples

Foxes often hunt at night. The foxes behind our house are surprisingly noisy.

  • Coffee keeps me awake.
  • The coffee you made is great.
  • Children learn through play.
  • The children in our group are ready.

What to watch for

  • The can refer to something shared or unique in context: “the moon”.
  • Many place names have no article, but use the with names such as the Netherlands and the Thames.
  • Some generic expressions use the, such as “play the violin”.

Your turn

Choose “Salt / The salt” in “___ in this soup makes it taste strong.”

Reveal the answer

The salt. You mean the salt in a particular soup.
