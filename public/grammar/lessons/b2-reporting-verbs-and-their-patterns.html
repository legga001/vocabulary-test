Reporting verbs and their patterns

Choose a verb that conveys the speaker’s intent. A verb such as advise takes a person plus to, admit can take -ing, and promise can take to.

The pattern

advise + person + to; admit + -ing; promise + to; suggest + -ing or that-clause

Work through an example

  1. The coach says “You ought to rest.”
  2. Choose advised because it was advice.
  3. Add the person and the action: “The coach advised me to rest.”

More examples

She advised me to wait. He admitted forgetting the key. He promised to bring it tomorrow.

  • She admitted leaving the door unlocked.
  • He refused to give up.
  • They warned us not to touch it.
  • Mara suggested taking a taxi.

What to watch for

  • Say “She suggested that I leave” or “She suggested leaving”. Avoid “suggested me to leave”.
  • Use an earlier action after admitted if the context is clear; “admitted having left” makes the order explicit.

Your turn

Report “I’ll send the link,” said Jo, using promised.

Reveal the answer

Jo promised to send the link. The person making the promise will do the action.
