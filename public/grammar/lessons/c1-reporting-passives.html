C1 grammar

Reporting passives

Use it is said that or someone is said to when the source is unspecified. Use to have plus a past participle when the reported event happened earlier.

Let’s talk it through

Mr Fox says This pattern lets you lead with the thing people are talking about. Work out when the reported event happened. If it came before the report, use to have plus a past participle to make that order clear.

The pattern

it + passive reporting verb + that-clause; subject + passive reporting verb + to-infinitive

How does it work?

  1. People believe that the painting is genuine.
  2. Make the painting the subject: “The painting is believed”.
  3. Add to be genuine: “The painting is believed to be genuine.”

More examples

It is believed that the bridge is safe. The fox is thought to have escaped through a gap.

  • It is reported that the road has reopened.
  • The road is reported to have reopened.
  • The workers are thought to be waiting outside.
  • The gate is said to have been damaged.

What to watch for

  • Use to have plus a participle to place a reported event earlier.
  • This wording leaves the source unnamed. When the source matters, use “Inspectors reported that…”.
