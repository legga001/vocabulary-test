B2 grammar

Concession and contrast

Use although or even though with a clause. Use despite or in spite of with a noun or an -ing form. Use however to connect contrasting statements.

The pattern

although/even though + subject and verb; despite/in spite of + noun or -ing; however starts a new sentence here

Work through an example

  1. The path was steep, but you kept walking.
  2. Use a full clause after although: “Although the path was steep, we kept walking.”
  3. Use a noun phrase after despite: “Despite the steep path, we kept walking.”

More examples

Although it rained, we played. Despite the rain, we played.

  • Even though it was late, we stayed.
  • In spite of feeling tired, Rae finished the race.
  • The lift was broken. However, we reached the top.

What to watch for

  • Say “despite the rain” without of. Keep of in “in spite of the rain”.
  • “Despite the fact that it rained” accepts a full clause after that.
  • Put a full stop before however when it links two complete sentences in this pattern.

Your turn

Rewrite “Although it was windy, we cycled” using despite.

Reveal the answer

Despite the wind, we cycled. Replace the full clause with a noun phrase.
