B2 grammar

Gerunds and infinitives with a change of meaning

Some verbs can take either form, with different meanings. Stop doing means end the action. Stop to do means pause one action in order to do another.

Let’s talk it through

Mr Fox says Read the sentence and imagine the order of events. Does remembering happen before the action or after it? Is trying an experiment or an effort? Those clues tell you which form fits.

The pattern

stop/remember/try + -ing or to + base verb, with different meanings

How does it work?

1. Compare stop doing with stop to do

  1. “I stopped drinking tea” means I ended that habit.
  2. “I stopped to drink tea” means I paused another activity.
  3. Ask yourself what stopped: the drinking, or what I was doing before it?

2. Compare remember doing with remember to do

“I remember locking the door” looks back. “Remember to lock the door” is a reminder about what to do later.

3. Compare try doing with try to do

“Try changing the bulb” gives a possible solution. “I tried to change it” tells us you made an effort.

More examples

I stopped running because I was tired. I stopped to tie my laces.

  • I remember posting the letter. I can picture doing it.
  • Remember to post the letter. Make sure you do it.
  • Try restarting the phone. See if that helps.
  • I tried to restart it. I made an effort.

What to watch for

  • Go on doing means continue. Go on to do means move to a new action.
  • Look at the meaning before choosing a form. The first verb alone doesn’t settle this group.
