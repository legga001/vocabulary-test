B1 grammar

Indirect questions

After a polite opening such as Could you tell me, use statement word order. Use if or whether for a yes-or-no question.

The pattern

question opening + question word or if/whether + subject + verb

Work through an example

  1. Begin with the direct question “Where are the keys?”
  2. Add “Do you know”, then put the subject before the verb inside the question.
  3. Say “Do you know where the keys are?”

More examples

Where is the station? → Could you tell me where the station is?

  • Could you tell me how much it costs?
  • Do you know if the lift works?
  • I wonder when the shops open.
  • Can you ask whether Luca is coming?

What to watch for

  • When who is already the subject, the order looks the same: “Do you know who called?”
  • End with a question mark if the whole sentence is a question. “I wonder where it is” can end with a full stop.

Your turn

Fix “Could you tell me where does this bus stop?”

Reveal the answer

Could you tell me where this bus stops? Use statement order inside the question.
