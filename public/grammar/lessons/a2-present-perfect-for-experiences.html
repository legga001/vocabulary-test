A2 grammar

Present perfect for experiences

Use have or has plus a past participle to talk about an experience without naming a finished time. Use the past simple when you say when it happened.

The pattern

have/has + past participle; past simple with a finished time

Work through an example

  1. Ask about someone’s life up to now: “Have you ever ridden a horse?”
  2. Use ridden, the past participle of ride.
  3. Once they give a time, switch to the past simple: “Yes, I rode one last summer.”

More examples

Have you ever seen a fox? I saw one last night.

  • I’ve never made sushi.
  • Has Nina read this book?
  • We’ve seen that film twice.
  • We saw it again on Friday.

What to watch for

  • Been can mean visited and returned. Gone can mean away now: “Lee has gone to the shop.”
  • Some participles are irregular: go → gone; write → written; see → seen.

Your turn

Choose: “I’ve tried / I tried that café last Tuesday.”

Reveal the answer

I tried that café last Tuesday. Last Tuesday names a finished time.
