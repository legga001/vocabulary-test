B2 grammar

Have or get something done

Use have or get plus an object and a past participle when you arrange for someone to do something for you.

Let’s talk it through

Mr Fox says Think of the object you hand over to someone who will do the work. Put that object after have or get, then say what happens to it.

The pattern

have/get + object + past participle; tense goes on have/get

How does it work?

  1. A mechanic will fix your bike tomorrow. You arranged it.
  2. Use “I’m having” for the arrangement.
  3. Say “I’m having my bike fixed tomorrow.”

More examples

I’m having my bike repaired. I got my hair cut yesterday.

  • We got the windows cleaned.
  • She has her eyes tested every year.
  • Can I get this jacket repaired?
  • They’ll have the boiler serviced.

What to watch for

  • Use the past participle after the object, even when the appointment is in the future.
  • Have can also describe an unwelcome event: “He had his wallet stolen.” Context tells you it wasn’t an arrangement.
