B2 grammar

Passive across tenses

Keep the event’s tense in the forms before the past participle. Use being for an action in progress, been after have, and be after a modal.

The pattern

continuous: be + being + participle; perfect: have + been + participle; modal: modal + be + participle

Work through an example

  1. Start with “They are fixing the lift.”
  2. Make the lift the subject and keep is for the current action.
  3. Add being fixed: “The lift is being fixed.”

More examples

The pitch is being repaired. The lights have been replaced. The match may be postponed.

  • The doors were being painted when I arrived.
  • The leak has been repaired.
  • All forms must be signed.
  • The result will be announced tomorrow.

What to watch for

  • A passive still needs the past participle of the main verb: “must be written”.
  • Perfect continuous passives are possible but often sound heavy. “They’ve been repairing it all week” is simpler.

Your turn

Rewrite “Someone has moved the chairs” starting with “The chairs”.

Reveal the answer

The chairs have been moved. The present perfect passive uses have been.
