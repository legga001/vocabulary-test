B1 grammar

Present and past passive

Use a form of be plus a past participle to focus on what receives the action. Mention the person doing it with by if it matters.

The pattern

am/is/are/was/were + past participle; by + person if useful

Work through an example

  1. Start with “Someone broke the window.”
  2. Make the window the subject. Choose was because broke is past and window is singular.
  3. Use broken: “The window was broken.”

More examples

The shirts are made in Leeds. The winning goal was scored by Alex.

  • The hall is cleaned every morning.
  • These shoes are made by hand.
  • The tickets were sold online.
  • Was the gate locked?

What to watch for

  • Use a verb that can take an object. “The baby slept” does not have one to move.
  • For a surprise event, say “A parcel arrived”, using the active form.
  • In “I was tired”, tired usually describes a state.

Your turn

Change “They painted the doors yesterday” to start with “The doors”.

Reveal the answer

The doors were painted yesterday. Doors is plural, so use were.
