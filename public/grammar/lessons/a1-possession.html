A1 grammar

Possession

Put my, your, his, her, its, our or their before a noun. Use mine or yours on its own. Add ’s to a name to show who owns something.

The pattern

owner + ’s + thing; my/your/etc. + noun; mine/yours/etc. alone

Work through an example

  1. Start with “The backpack belongs to Ella.”
  2. Put Ella first and add ’s: “It’s Ella’s backpack.”
  3. After we know the object, use “It’s hers.”

More examples

That’s Mia’s scarf. Her scarf is green. The blue one is mine.

  • That’s our table. The window seat is ours.
  • The twins’ coats are wet.
  • The dog chased its tail.

What to watch for

  • Put only an apostrophe after a regular plural ending in s: the girls’ room.
  • Its shows possession. It’s is short for it is or it has.
  • Use “her phone” and “the phone is hers”.

Your turn

Replace “my pencil” in “That’s my pencil” with one word.

Reveal the answer

That’s mine. Mine can stand alone.
