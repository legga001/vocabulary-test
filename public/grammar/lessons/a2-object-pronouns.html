A2 grammar

Object pronouns

Use me, you, him, her, it, us and them after a verb or preposition.

Let’s talk it through

Mr Fox says Look at the action and ask “Who receives it?” The person doing the action can be I or she, but the person after a verb or preposition is often me or her.

The pattern

verb or preposition + me/you/him/her/it/us/them

How does it work?

1. Replace the object of an action

  1. Start with “Mina saw Alex and me.”
  2. You are talking about Alex and yourself, so choose us.
  3. Say “Mina saw us.”

2. Replace a noun after a preposition

Start with “This seat is for Ella.” Replace Ella with her → “This seat is for her.”

3. Keep the subject and object forms apart

Use I before the action and me after another person’s action → “I called Jo. Jo called me.”

More examples

Please call me. I saved a seat for her.

  • I called him yesterday.
  • Pass it to me, please.
  • Can you come with us?
  • I saw them at the market.

What to watch for

  • Use I as the person doing the action and me as the person receiving it: “I helped Mo. Mo helped me.”
  • You and it have the same form in both positions.
