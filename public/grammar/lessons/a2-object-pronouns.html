A2 grammar

Object pronouns

Use me, you, him, her, it, us and them after a verb or preposition.

The pattern

verb or preposition + me/you/him/her/it/us/them

Work through an example

  1. Start with “Mina saw Alex and me.”
  2. You are talking about Alex and yourself, so choose us.
  3. Say “Mina saw us.”

More examples

Please call me. I saved a seat for her.

  • I called him yesterday.
  • Pass it to me, please.
  • Can you come with us?
  • I saw them at the market.

What to watch for

  • Use I as the person doing the action and me as the person receiving it: “I helped Mo. Mo helped me.”
  • You and it have the same form in both positions.

Your turn

Replace “the twins” in “I made lunch for the twins.”

Reveal the answer

I made lunch for them. Use them after for.
