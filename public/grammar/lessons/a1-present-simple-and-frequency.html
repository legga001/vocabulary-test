A1 grammar

Present simple and frequency

Use the present simple for habits and facts. Add -s or -es with he, she and it. Put usually or often before most verbs, but after be.

Let’s talk it through

Mr Fox says To describe a normal day, start with the action and add how often it happens. Pay special attention when the person doing the action is one other person, like she or he.

The pattern

I/we/you/they + base verb; he/she/it + verb ending in s or es

How does it work?

1. Describe a habit with he, she or it

  1. Start with “I cycle to work.”
  2. Change I to Ava. Ava is she, so add s to cycle.
  3. Put usually before the verb: “Ava usually cycles to work.”

2. Talk about a habit with I, you, we or they

Start with we and use the plain verb walk → “We walk home every day.”

3. Put frequency words after be

Start with “Mina is late.” Put often after is → “Mina is often late.”

4. Change the ending when needed

With she, change study to studies → “She studies every evening.”

More examples

Sam usually walks to school. He’s often early.

  • I often read on the bus.
  • Noah watches cartoons on Sundays.
  • Bees make honey.
  • The shop is always busy.

What to watch for

  • Study becomes studies. Play becomes plays.
  • Have becomes has. Go becomes goes.
  • Put often after be: “They are often early.”
