A1 grammar

Present continuous

Use am, is or are plus an -ing verb for an action happening now. Compare the action with a habit in the present simple.

The pattern

am/is/are + verb ending in -ing

Work through an example

  1. You hear the kettle right now.
  2. Choose is for the kettle.
  3. Add boiling: “The kettle is boiling.”

More examples

I play every Saturday. I’m playing right now!

  • I’m making soup.
  • They aren’t listening.
  • Are you waiting for me?
  • We’re staying with a friend this week.

What to watch for

  • Drop the final e in make → making. Double the last letter in run → running.
  • For a feeling or state, use “I know the answer” and “I want lunch”.

Your turn

Choose: “Look! The bus comes / is coming.”

Reveal the answer

The bus is coming. Look tells us the action is happening now.
