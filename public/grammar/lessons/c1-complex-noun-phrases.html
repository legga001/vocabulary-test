C1 grammar

Complex noun phrases

Put key information near the noun it describes. A following phrase can narrow the meaning without piling several modifiers in front of the noun.

The pattern

determiner + modifiers + main noun + after-noun detail

Work through an example

  1. Start with “the report”.
  2. Add the subject of the report after the noun: “the report on flood risks”.
  3. Show which risks: “the report on flood risks in coastal towns”.

More examples

The report on flood risks in coastal towns arrives on Friday.

  • The three red boxes on the shelf.
  • An unusually detailed guide to local wildlife.
  • The files that arrived yesterday.
  • A two-week course in creative writing.

What to watch for

  • Find the main noun to choose the verb: “The list of names is ready” uses is because list is singular.
  • A number plus a unit before the noun usually keeps the unit singular: “a two-week course”.
  • Move a long modifier after the noun if it reads more clearly there.

Your turn

Choose is or are: “The box of spare cables ___ under the desk.”

Reveal the answer

Is. Box is the main noun, while of spare cables gives extra detail.
