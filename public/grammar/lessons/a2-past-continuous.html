A2 grammar

Past continuous

Use was or were plus -ing for an action in progress in the past. Use the past simple for a completed event that happened during it.

Let’s talk it through

Mr Fox says Picture a short event happening in the middle of a longer action. Use was or were plus -ing for the longer action, and the past simple for the event that interrupts it.

The pattern

was/were + -ing for the action in progress; past simple for a finished event

How does it work?

1. Show a short event interrupting a longer one

  1. Picture Ali in the middle of washing the dishes.
  2. Give the background with “Ali was washing the dishes”.
  3. Add the event: “Ali was washing the dishes when the doorbell rang.”

2. Show two actions in progress

Use was cooking and was setting → “While Jo was cooking, Eli was setting the table.”

3. Show a moment in the past

Choose were waiting to show the wait was in progress → “At nine, we were still waiting.”

More examples

I was walking home when it started to rain.

  • At midnight, we were still driving.
  • While I was cooking, Kim was setting the table.
  • It was getting dark when we left.
  • Were you sleeping when I called?

What to watch for

  • Use the past simple for steps in a finished sequence: “I opened the door and turned on the light.”
  • State verbs often use the simple form: “I knew the answer.”
