A2 grammar

Past simple

Use the past simple for a finished action. Many verbs take -ed, while others change form. After did or didn’t, switch back to the base verb.

The pattern

subject + past verb; did/didn’t + base verb

Work through an example

  1. Start with “I walk to school.”
  2. Add ed to walk for yesterday: “I walked to school.”
  3. An irregular verb changes in its own way: “I went to school.”

More examples

We watched the match. Leo went home early. Did he miss the final goal?

  • We stopped for lunch.
  • Ada bought a new lamp.
  • They didn’t come back.
  • What did you eat?

What to watch for

  • Try becomes tried, and stop becomes stopped.
  • Use “Did you go?”, with go after did.
  • Be has its own past forms, was and were.

Your turn

Fix “We didn’t saw the ending.”

Reveal the answer

We didn’t see the ending. Use the base form after didn’t.
