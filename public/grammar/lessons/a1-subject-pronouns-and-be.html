A1 grammar

Subject pronouns and be

Use I am, he/she/it is, and you/we/they are. Put not after be to make a negative. For a question, move be before the subject.

The pattern

I + am / he, she, it + is / you, we, they + are

Work through an example

  1. Start with “The dogs are wet.” → The dogs means they.
  2. Add not after are to say the opposite: “The dogs aren’t wet.”
  3. Move are to the front to ask: “Are the dogs wet?”

More examples

I’m ready. They aren’t late. Is she at home?

  • I’m in the kitchen.
  • We’re from York.
  • It isn’t my turn.
  • Where are your glasses?

What to watch for

  • You takes are even when you speak to one person.
  • In a short positive answer, say “Yes, I am”, rather than “Yes, I’m”.

Your turn

Make “Maya is ready” into a question.

Reveal the answer

Is Maya ready? Move is before Maya.
