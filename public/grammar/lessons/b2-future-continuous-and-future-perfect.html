B2 grammar

Future continuous and future perfect

Use will be plus -ing for an action in progress at a future time. Use will have plus a past participle for something completed by a future deadline.

The pattern

will be + -ing at a future time; will have + past participle by a future time

Work through an example

  1. Think of the journey at 9 a.m.: you’re in the middle of it.
  2. Say “At nine, we’ll be driving.”
  3. Think of noon, your planned arrival deadline: “By noon, we’ll have arrived.”

More examples

At nine I’ll be travelling. By noon I’ll have arrived.

  • This time tomorrow, I’ll be swimming.
  • Will you be using the car at six?
  • By Friday, Maya will have sent the report.
  • The library won’t have opened by then.

What to watch for

  • By means on or before a deadline. Until describes how long an activity or state lasts.
  • Future continuous can ask about plans politely without directly requesting a change.

Your turn

Choose for a finished deadline: “By Tuesday, they’ll be painting / they’ll have painted the door.”

Reveal the answer

They’ll have painted the door. The painting will be complete by Tuesday.
